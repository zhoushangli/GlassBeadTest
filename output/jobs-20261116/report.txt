Behavior: dog anchor scoring reads one old-anchor snapshot, preserving coverage penalty; target claims remain centralized. Shared coverage counts are computed once per selection round, subtracting the dog's own old anchor. Sheep results use packed batch columns. RNG remains on the main thread in stable order. JobService collects completions and invokes concrete jobs' apply_result at the stage barrier. Reset/shutdown reclaim tasks and discard unapplied results.

The serial control includes the same new dog rule, shared-coverage preparation and packed sheep results. Its difference from parallel isolates scheduling; it is not the original implementation. jobs_original_baseline is one earlier observational run and is not a controlled estimate of total before/after improvement.

Earlier/pre-packed all-stage runs and 2/4/8 worker probes are retained under output/performance and source snapshots under pre-packed. Eight-way scheduling was slower. These earlier versions are not substituted for final measurements.

Wall-clock runs advance different numbers of simulation steps and can have different surviving sheep counts; exact per-step serial/parallel state and RNG equivalence is verified separately. GPU time is unavailable. Automated screenshots/input checks do not establish natural movement or human visual acceptance.
